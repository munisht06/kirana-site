Privacy policy

Kirana: Family Groceries · Effective October 26, 2026

Kirana is a grocery list for one family at a time. We collect only what the app needs to work. We don't sell data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account: When you sign in with Apple we receive an account identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email (possibly an Apple private relay address). We store the display name you choose.
  • Family content: your family's name, member list and roles, grocery items (name, quantity, category, brand, notes, optional price limits), pickup requests and replies, prices shoppers record, saved store names, and shopping status.
  • Notifications: if you turn on alerts, a push token for your device so we can deliver family updates.

Items you keep under “Items on this iPhone” stay on your device and are not sent to us.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, analytics or crash-tracking SDKs.

How it's used and shared

Your family content is visible to members of your family. Pickup requests are visible to the requester and the chosen shopper. Notifications show only a generic message (“Your family has a grocery update”); details appear after you open the app. We use these service providers to run Kirana: Google Firebase (sign-in and push delivery), Apple Push Notification service, Cloudflare (API hosting) and Neon (database hosting). They process data only to provide those services.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your family uses Kirana. You can delete your account anytime in the app (Account → Delete account). This removes you from your family, cancels your open requests, revokes your notification devices and disables your account. Items you added to a shared list stay for the remaining members, attributed to “Deleted member”. If you're the only member, your family and its list are deleted. Deleting the app also removes on-device data.

Children

Kirana is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page if our practices change. For privacy questions or requests, open a request (without private details) and we'll respond.
