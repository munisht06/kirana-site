Kirana

Family groceries, shared

Kirana keeps one grocery list for your family, lets you ask whoever is out to pick something up (with an optional price limit), and lets family members say when they're shopping.

Getting started

  1. Sign in with Apple.
  2. Create your family, or join one with an invitation code from the family owner (Account → Manage family).
  3. Add groceries on the Groceries tab. Everyone in your family sees the same list.
  4. To ask someone to pick things up, open Requests → Ask for a pickup. Optional: “Only buy if…” sets a price limit.
  5. When you head out, open Shopping → I'm shopping so your family knows.

Common questions

I can't send a pickup request.

A request goes to another family member. Invite someone from Account → Manage family first.

I'm not getting alerts.

Open Requests → Inbox → Enable pickup notifications, and check Settings → Notifications → Kirana. Everything also appears in the in-app Inbox.

Does Kirana use my location?

No. Shopping status is only what you choose to share, and it expires on its own.

How do I delete my account?

Account → Delete account. You leave your family and your open requests are canceled. If you're the only member, your family and its list are deleted. If you own a family with other members, transfer ownership first.
