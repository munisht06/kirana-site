Kirana

Family groceries, shared

Kirana keeps one grocery list for your family. Check items off as they go in your cart so nobody buys them twice, ask whoever is out to pick something up, and let your family know when you're shopping.

Getting started

  1. Sign in with Apple.
  2. Create your family, or join one with a family link or an invitation code.
  3. Add groceries on the list, or tap a quick-add such as Gas or Milk. Everyone in your family sees the same list.
  4. When you head out, tap I'm going shopping. Tap each item as it goes in your cart, then tap Done shopping.
  5. To ask someone to pick things up, tap Ask, choose the person, tick the items and send.

Common questions

How do I invite my family?

Open Account, then Manage family. Share a family link, or create a short invitation code. If you remove someone, the family link resets, so share the new one with anyone still joining.

I can't ask anyone for a pickup.

Asks go to another family member. Invite someone first.

I'm not getting alerts.

Tap the bell on the list and turn on notifications, then check Settings, Notifications, Kirana. Updates also appear in the in-app Inbox.

Does Kirana use my location?

No. Shopping status is only what you choose to share, and it expires on its own.

How do I delete my account?

Account, then Delete account. You leave your family and your open asks are canceled. If you're the only member, your family and its list are deleted. If you own a family with other members, transfer ownership or delete the family first.
